Known limitations
Endpoint loopback includes other application audio. No process loopback, custom AEC, AssemblyAI or DeepSeek Direct adapter. Infer needs an externally mixed source; heuristic turn/state extraction is conservative, not a full dialogue understanding model. Sparse English-word retrieval may be weak for languages without word separators. Token estimates are deliberately conservative. UI render callback and RMS energy are measurement proxies. Restart session after C transport failure or exhausted STT retries. Local notes/Summary are plaintext application-data files; audio and transcript are not persisted unless debug content logging is explicitly enabled (debug logs contain text, not audio).
